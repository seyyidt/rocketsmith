In one sentence

Build rockets from parts, launch them off a beach and sink a cartoon navy, farther and farther out at sea.

Description

Rocketsmith is a physics game about engineering. You start with a firecracker on a rail and a pile of cash. Every ship you hit pays, and the farther out it sails, the more it's worth. Spend the money on bigger engines, more fuel and smarter warheads, and push farther out: from rafts a few hundred meters off the beach to the Baron's sea fortress 70 km away.

Every flight is simulated. A rocket that's too heavy falls short; one with its fins in the wrong place flips over, and the workshop shows you why. Stack warheads, tanks, engines, fins and separators into multi-stage rockets with strap-on boosters, build jet and ramjet cruise missiles that skim the waves, or fly through space and survive re-entry.

Seventeen contracts from the generals tell the story, and a job board hands out a new job for every one you finish. The enemy is the fictional Baron von Kaboom, and every crew escapes in its lifeboats.

Features

  • 59 rocket parts and 10 launch sites, from a beach rail to a missile silo
  • Multi-stage rockets, strap-on boosters, cruise missiles with wings and an autopilot
  • Cluster bombs, torpedoes, decoys, re-entry vehicles and MIRVs
  • A built-in illustrated guide that explains thrust, stability and staging
  • 17 contracts, a job board that never runs dry, and free flight over an endless ocean
  • A kill cam for every hit
  • Original soundtrack and sound effects, composed in code
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no timers. Plays offline.
