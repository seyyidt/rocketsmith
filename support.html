Common questions

My rocket flips over right after launch.

It's unstable: in the workshop the red dot (lift) has to sit below the yellow dot (weight). Add bigger fins at the bottom, or move heavy parts like the warhead and tanks higher.

I'm out of money.

You can't go broke: the government tops your funds back up to your rank's allowance after every flight. Every flight also pays for distance, hit or miss.

How do I unlock more contracts and parts?

Contracts give reputation (★). Reputation raises your rank, and each rank reveals new contracts and lets you research new parts and launch sites in the workshop.

My cruise missile won't start.

A ramjet only lights above about 370 km/h, so put a booster stage or strap-ons under it. Jet and rocket engines can't share a stage; put a separator between them.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is stored on your device only. It comes along when you restore your iPhone or iPad from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the gear button at the top of the main screen. Sound and music each have their own switch.
