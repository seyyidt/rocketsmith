Full screen on iPhone

Safari on iPhone cannot show a page full screen, but the home screen can:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Start Rocketsmith from its new icon. It opens without the browser's bars.

The game from the home screen keeps its own progress. What you earned here in Safari stays in Safari.
