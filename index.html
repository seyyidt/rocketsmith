How it plays

1Build

Engineer every stage

Warheads, tanks, engines, fins, wings, separators and strap-on boosters, from a black powder motor to the 450 kN Titan. The workshop shows thrust, Δv and where weight and lift sit, so you can see why a rocket flips, and fix it. Turn the rocket around in 3D while you build.

2Learn

Rocket science, explained

Never built a rocket? The built-in guide explains thrust, fuel, stability and staging in twelve short illustrated articles, with pictures of the real parts. You will know what TWR means by lunchtime.

3Sneak

Cruise missiles

Jet engines, ramjets, wings and an autopilot that holds your height. Boost straight up, let the gas thrusters turn the missile level, then skim the waves under the flak and slip past the close-in guns.

4Climb

All the way to space

Above 25 km the sky turns black and nothing slows you down. Stage your way up past the satellites, then bring a re-entry vehicle back through the heat and down onto a ship.

5Overdo it

Warheads for every occasion

Cluster pods, torpedoes that swim the last stretch, penetrators for armor, a MIRV bus with three guided warheads, and one bomb the Baron would rather you never research. The crews always make it to the lifeboats.

6Fly

You are the guidance system

Hold a finger where the rocket should go, or use the steer buttons. A map that zooms by itself shows the fleet ahead, and the chase camera pulls back until you see the whole sea below.

7Lock on

See what the rocket sees

Fit a TV Seeker and one button switches the screen to the camera in the nose: grey sea, white ships. Drag to look around, zoom in, tap a ship, and the rocket flies itself onto it.
