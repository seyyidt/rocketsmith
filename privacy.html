ROCKETSMITH

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

Rocketsmith does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Rocketsmith is made by Seyyid Tiryaki, who is the controller in the sense of the GDPR. Contact: seyyid.tiryaki@gmail.com.

The apps (iPhone, iPad, Android, PC)

  • The game runs entirely on your device and works offline. It makes no network connections.
  • Your progress (money, rank, rockets and settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • The stores have their own rules. Apple and Google may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with developers if you allow it in your device's settings. On Steam, Steam Cloud can copy your save file to your Steam account so it follows you to another PC; you can turn that off in Steam. This is between you and the store, under the store's privacy policy.

The web demo and this website

  • The demo saves your progress in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it.
  • This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It loads no fonts, scripts or pictures from other servers.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, the host receives your IP address to deliver the page and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and to keep the service running (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have no access to these logs. See the host's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.
  • If you write us an email, we use your address and message only to answer you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. We hold none, apart from emails you sent us. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is rated 13+. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if the game gets an online feature), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: seyyid.tiryaki@gmail.com.
